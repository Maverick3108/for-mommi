My Siddhi,

I know you. Not the version the world gets to see, but the one underneath it all. The girl who notices the moment someone goes quiet. The girl who fights battles at home that nobody claps for. The girl who loves so completely and never once asks to be thanked for it.

The other night I started thinking about you as a mother one day. I meant to write down a few things. It turned into a hundred, and honestly, I could have kept going.

Every card in here is something true about you. Not a compliment to make you smile. Not a guess. Just you, exactly the way I see you.

So take your time. Turn them slowly. Let yourself believe every single one.

Forever yours,Varu

Siddhi,

You might wonder how I could know all of this when we have barely shared a room. But I have been paying attention. In every late night call squeezed into the only hours we have. In every time you stayed on the line through something hard instead of hanging up. In every time you laughed at something silly on a day that had been heavy.

I know the girl who takes care of everyone and asks for nothing back. I know the girl who is fighting, quietly, every single day, for a little more room to breathe. I know how much you feel, and how much of it you have had to carry by yourself.

You do not have to carry it by yourself anymore.

One day there will be tiny hands holding yours. And they will never know a single day without being loved the way you love. They will be free in all the ways you had to fight for. They will be gentle and brave, just like their mother.

And on the days you doubt yourself, I want you to come back here and read these again. Not because I wrote them. Because every single one of them is true.

I see you. I always have. I always will.

All of me, always,Varu
